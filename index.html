The system
What it does

A single coherent pipeline: from market scan to signal, sizing, execution, compliance and continuous learning — with full observability the whole way.

📡

Scans the market

Sweeps 31+ instruments (FX, indices, metals, crypto, oil) every 15 minutes across multiple timeframes.

🧠

24 strategies

Institutional playbooks — ICT, SMC, multi-timeframe, AMD, order blocks, FVG — scored into a single conviction signal.

⚡

Auto-executes

Places trades with multi-stage exits (TP1/TP2/TP3 + ATR trailing) and reconciles state directly with MT5.

🛡️

Protects capital

A 13-layer risk stack: conviction & volatility sizing, loss-streak halts, drawdown brakes, auto-blacklisting.

🏦

Broker-aware

Auto-detects 27 broker / prop-firm profiles (FTMO, FundingPips, The5ers…) and enforces each one's rules.

🔁

Learns continuously

Pattern memory + an ML score adjuster refine decisions from every closed trade; underperformers self-quarantine.

Depth
Engineered capabilities

Not a script — a platform. Multi-account orchestration, statistical validation and enterprise-grade operations.

🛡️ Risk management — 13 layers

  • Pre-trade gates: score threshold, R:R, news mute, regime alignment
  • Dynamic sizing: conviction + volatility + loss-streak governor
  • Daily soft-stop, total-drawdown hard-kill, drawdown-velocity brake
  • Symbol & strategy auto-blacklisting on rolling underperformance

🤖 AI & machine learning

  • LLM market analyst — daily briefings & A+ pre-trade consults
  • Conversational AI co-pilot over the live system state
  • Breaking-news reaction from RSS + Claude analysis
  • Sklearn score adjuster + pattern-memory self-learning

📊 Validation & rigor

  • Walk-forward backtesting & out-of-sample evaluation
  • 10,000-run Monte Carlo robustness simulation
  • Parameter-sensitivity sweeps & Bayesian edge analysis
  • 265 unit & integration tests across critical paths

🌐 Operations & observability

  • Multi-tenant orchestration — personal & corporate capital, per-client investor reporting
  • Web dashboard, PWA mobile app, Telegram alerts, Cloudflare tunnel
  • 52-endpoint REST API + Prometheus metrics
  • Tamper-proof audit log, self-healing supervisor, heartbeat
